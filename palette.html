Palette benchmark and candidates

Six palettes built on the same structure, so the v4 screens can be compared one for one. Every candidate keeps the warm paper ground and the single-action-colour rule — only the brand hue and the accent move.

The mockup carries a palette switcher in its top bar. Open any screen and flip the six options there; the choice is stored in the browser and can be shared as a URL (?theme=navy).

What the benchmark says

Green and teal are not the category default. Of the nine sites sampled below, only two lead with a teal-family brand colour — treatment.com (#03A989) and plasticsurgery.org (#018BAF). Both are high-volume, utility-first properties. They read as “generic healthcare provider”, the colour of hospital portals and pharmacy chains.

The dominant pattern in this category is a deep ink or navy brand plane with a warm metallic accent. The closest match to our own reference — a premium Korean clinic’s global site — uses #1A1F2E ink + #C9A962 gold on #EDE8E0 cream. Navy & Brass carries the same structure pushed further into blue, which reads more institutional and less luxury — and, for a directory, the better promise.

Decision: Navy & Brass, confirmed on 22 Oct 2026. Its brass passes: #A8802F at 3.26:1 on the canvas for large fills and #8A6524 at 4.77:1 for small text, so the accent can do both jobs. Navy also states what this product actually is — a registry you can check — rather than a luxury clinic you might visit.

Unchanged in v4. The palette is not what this revision is about. What changed is the data: every number on every v4 screen is now measured, and every price carries its source and the date it was read. See the coverage page.
