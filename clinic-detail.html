What this page does not tell you

Four things a price list cannot answer.

  • Whether this is the right clinic for you. We do not rank quality and we have not read a single patient review.
  • What the final price will be. Lines with a unit — cc, shots, units — depend on the amount your doctor decides on.
  • Whether the price still holds. We read it once. It is re-read weekly, but a clinic can change its list between reads.
  • Who will treat you, when you can be seen, or whether the clinic will answer a message in English.
