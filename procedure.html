How to read these numbers

Three things change what a Botox figure means, and we do not hide any of them.

Unit Clinics price per unit (U), per area, or per package. ₩30,000 for one area and ₩30,000 for a course are not the same thing, so we show the clinic’s own wording on the detail page rather than normalising it.
Area Wrinkle area, jawline, calf, underarm and full face are priced separately, and a full-face line is usually cheaper per unit than a single area.
Promotion A clinic that labels a line as a first-visit or event price publishes a lower number. We keep those but mark them, and we exclude them when we take the middle figure for a clinic.
Package A line that covers several treatments at once — a device plus a filler, or a course of sessions — is not a price for this treatment, so we leave it out of every range here. How many clinics that leaves out is on the coverage page.

What we cannot tell you about Botox prices

  • Which clinic will give the best result. We have not read a review and we do not rank quality.
  • What the price will be for you. The unit, the area and the amount are decided at consultation.
  • Whether a clinic’s lowest figure is its normal price or a promotion. We mark the ones the clinic itself labels as a promotion; we cannot catch an unlabelled one.
  • What the price was before or after 24 October 2026. We read it once and re-read weekly, so anything the clinic changed between reads is not on the page.
