The price re-read takes 51 seconds for all 609 known sites — we measured it. Korean clinic prices change roughly once or twice a year, so a weekly re-read is comfortably ahead of them.

How we look for the 1,778 clinics that have no website

Rules only. No model reads a page and guesses.

1 · Look the clinic up. We ask a search API for the clinic by name, restricted to the district, with the registry’s own coordinates as the centre point.
2 · Check the address. We take the registry’s coordinates and the search result’s coordinates and compare. If they are within 40 metres, it is the same building.
3 · Check the name or the telephone number. One of the two has to match as well. Both filters have to pass before we accept a URL.
4 · Read the site for prices. Only once a URL is accepted do we open the site and look for a treatment with an amount.
5 · Remember it. An accepted URL is written to a registry file and never searched for again. A clinic that failed is put on a backoff — one month, then three, then six, then it stops.
measured We ran the full pipeline on 36 clinics in two runs. 35 of the 36 produced a URL candidate, 16 passed the address and name checks, and 1 of those confirmed sites had a treatment price we could read. That last number is the honest one: finding the website does not mean the clinic publishes a price. The second run stopped after 6 clinics because the monthly search allowance ran out, so the sample is small.
not published Two requests per clinic, 3,556 requests for the 1,778, about 94 minutes on one machine. At the search plan’s rate that is $17.78 for a full sweep — and it does not need to be repeated, because every answer is cached.
projection Across the whole pool, 46 of 2,387 clinics publish a price we can read — 1.9%. Applied to the 1,778, that is roughly 34 more clinics with a readable price, for $17.78 of search. The sweep also produces a correct website for about 790 of them, which is worth having even where no price follows.
!
The search quota is currently spent

Our search plan includes $5 of free requests a month — 1,000 requests. We used it, and the API is now refusing further calls until the month resets. A full sweep needs 3,556 requests, so it fits inside the free allowance only if it is spread over about 3.6 months of 1,000 requests each; otherwise it needs a paid top-up. We are not publishing clinics we have not actually reached — a quota wall is a reason to wait, not a reason to guess.

Why parking and Saturday hours are thin

Only 284 of the 2,387 clinics have a detailed registry record at all — the file that carries parking spaces and Saturday times. The other 2,103 have a name, an address and an opening date, and nothing more. We checked the detailed-record API as well: of the 978 records it returns, every clinic that carries any of these fields is already inside that 284. It adds no coverage we did not have.

So the two thin filters are a gap in the source, not in our collection. We still offer them, and we show “not in the registry” as its own option rather than leaving you to wonder whether a missing value means no parking or no record.

How we treat a clinic’s site

We audit the rules on every site before we collect from it.

RuleWhat we found across 585 hostsWhat we do
robots.txt 328 hosts publish one · 257 return 404 We read it and follow it. 53 hosts disallow everything.
Disallow: / 53 hosts (9%) We do not collect from these at all.
Crawl-delay 25 hosts specify one · median 10s · max 3,600s We wait exactly as long as they ask. At 3,600s that is one visit a day.
Price paths 4 hosts disallow their price path We skip the price page on those four.
Identification All of our requests We send our own user-agent with a contact URL, and we keep at least one second between visits to the same host.

What a price on this site is

The definition we hold ourselves to.

own page The figure is on the clinic’s own website, in the clinic’s own wording. We do not estimate, convert, average or adjust it.
promotion The clinic itself labels the line as a first-visit or event price. We keep it and mark it, and we leave it out when we take a clinic’s middle figure.
not published The clinic is in the registry but publishes no amount we can read. It gets no price on this site, and no guess.

Units are not normalised. A clinic that lists ₩50,000 per 100U and a clinic that lists ₩500,000 for a full face are both correct at what they publish — we show both and label the unit.

What we cannot tell you

The list is longer than the page. This is the honest version of it.

  • Quality. We have not read a single patient review. Nothing on this site ranks a clinic by how good it is.
  • Price for you. The unit, the area and the amount are decided at consultation. A published figure is a starting point, not a quote.
  • Which clinic is good value. We can tell you what a clinic charges. We cannot tell you whether the result will be worth it, and we do not score clinics.
  • Every clinic. 2,341 of the 2,387 clinics in the three districts are not listed. They publish no price we can read. See what we do list →
  • Real-time prices. We re-read weekly. Between reads a clinic can change its list, and we would not know until the next read.
  • Doctor, hours, language, reception. The registry does not hold these. We do not print them for a clinic we have not verified, so for most clinics on this site they are blank.
  • Whether a price is the final one. Prices may be quoted before VAT, and packages work out at a different effective rate. The clinic confirms at consultation.
