How this directory works

We do not buy listings and we do not publish a price a clinic has not published. A clinic appears here only because we read a price off its own site.

1

Registry record

Name, address, coordinates, opening date and telephone come from the national medical facility dataset. Nothing here is entered by hand.

2

The clinic's own price page

We open the clinic's website and read its published non-covered treatment prices. The page we read is linked on every price.

3

Re-checked every week

Prices are re-read weekly and the page shows the date they were read. When a clinic changes a price, the old figure disappears.

i
Why the list is short

2,387 clinics sit in the registry for these three districts. Only 44 publish a price we can read. We would rather show 44 real ones than 2,387 rows with nothing in them. And 44 is a number that moves — the search sweep that looks for the remaining 1,778 clinics runs on a timer, so this count grows as it goes.

!
Not every listing can take your enquiry

Where we could not confirm a working contact channel, the button says Ask about this area and the request is routed to a clinic nearby. We do not pass an enquiry to a clinic we could not reach.

What we cannot tell you

Honest limits, stated up front.

  • Patient reviews and before/after photos are not included in this version.
  • Booking and payment are not handled here — this site only introduces you.
  • English-speaking status is not published, because we do not verify it by phone yet.
  • 2,387 minus 44 clinics are in the registry but are not listed here. Most of them publish no price we can read; some of them we have not found a website for yet, and the search sweep is still working through those. We say why on the coverage page.
  • Final price is always confirmed at consultation.
