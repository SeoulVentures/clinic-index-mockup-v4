What we cannot tell you about Sinsa

  • Which of the 13 is best. We do not rank quality and we have not read a single patient review.
  • What a treatment costs for you. Units differ between clinics and the amount is decided at consultation.
  • Whether a clinic still has a website. 609 of the 2,387 clinics in the three districts had a website on file; 1,778 did not.
  • Whether these prices are current at the moment you read them. We re-read weekly, not continuously.
